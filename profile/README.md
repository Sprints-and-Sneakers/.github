### ⚡ Engineers behind the growth

> There are over **10,000** marketing agencies in Europe.
> There is only **one** that combines **sprint-based experimentation**, **AI-native execution** and a **B Corp certification**.

Behind every experiment sits code: tracking foundations, API integrations, automations and AI agents that turn a winning test into a system that runs on its own. This is where we build it.

### 👟 Our culture

We wear sneakers, not suits.

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Young, ambitious, no-nonsense team
- 📚 *Personal growth is in our constitution*: the last Friday of every month is dedicated to development
- 💡 **Core values:** Honesty · Learning · Respect · Ownership · Relationships · Memorable · Hard Work
- 🎉 **Benefits:** co-ownership (share program), profit sharing, internal Academy
- 🌱 We exist to build a world where **growth and impact are inseparable** and people enjoy life
